What is the significance of Surah Al-Fatiha?
10:31 AM
Surah Al-Fatiha, "The Opening," is the first chapter of the Quran and one of the most profound. It is called Umm al-Quran (Mother of the Quran) and is recited in every unit of prayer — making it the most repeated supplication in Islam.

It covers three core themes: praise and gratitude to Allah, acknowledgment of His sovereignty on the Day of Judgment, and a heartfelt supplication for guidance on the straight path.
